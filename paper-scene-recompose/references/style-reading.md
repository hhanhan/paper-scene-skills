# Style Reading

## Purpose

Extract an abstract visual grammar from one or more style exemplars while preventing their subject matter from leaking into a new artwork. The source photograph supplies meaning; the Style Profile supplies only page behavior and material logic. Neither the source nor style exemplars enter the generation call.

## Style Fingerprint

Read each exemplar across stable axes:

- **page topology**: asymmetry, focal placement, directional axes, counterweights, and active quiet space;
- **motif hierarchy**: focal form, relational echo, environmental field, counterform, and their scale differences;
- **shape language**: cut, torn, brushed, stamped, drawn, geometric, organic, or eroded forms;
- **edge character**: crisp cut, exposed fiber, dry breakup, ink bleed, overlap, misregistration, or abrasion;
- **material process**: which paper and print processes appear and which one dominates;
- **color architecture**: stock tone, dominant ink, companion tones, signal color, and each color's structural purpose;
- **density rhythm**: distribution of fine marks, medium structures, broad masses, and silence;
- **depth order**: the sequence of paper field, printed atmosphere, main forms, overprint, and optional type;
- **semantic distance**: how observation becomes metaphor, spatial behavior, or material behavior without literal reconstruction;
- **type relationship**: scale, position, and print voice only, never copied wording.

Across several exemplars, preserve recurring properties and discard isolated subject-specific choices. With one exemplar, keep uncertainty explicit and avoid converting every detail into a rule.

## Content-Leak Guard

Never carry over an exemplar's people, landmarks, objects, symbols, poses, narrative, exact crop, exact layout, wording, signature, watermark, or brand identity. Never request the style of a named living artist. Rewrite retained traits as neutral operations that still make sense when the exemplar subject is removed.

## Direction-Engine Handoff

This document produces the neutral Style Profile only. It does not construct or select directions. Hand the completed Profile and sealed Evidence Capsule to [direction-engine.md](direction-engine.md), which exclusively owns candidate fingerprints, hard gates, selection, runner-up retention, and failure routing.

For auditability, store each Style Fingerprint property with its supporting visual evidence, confidence, stability across exemplars, and exclusions. Do not expose this internal record unless the user asks.

## Prompt Translation

Translate the selected direction into visible placement and material behavior. Name what occupies the foreground, middle scale, and atmospheric field; describe how one motif echoes or resists another; identify where paper remains quiet and why. Avoid vague mood words unless paired with a visual mechanism.

Never pass the source photograph, benchmark results, or style exemplars to `image_gen`. Send the complete text-only Blueprint and abstract Style Profile.
