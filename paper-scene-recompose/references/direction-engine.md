# Direction Engine

## Evidence Ledger

Before proposing directions, record the observable source cues, user requirements, stable Style Profile properties, interpretations, confidence, and exclusions. Every abstract form in the final direction must trace back to this ledger. Do not treat an uncertain interpretation as fact.

## Three Structurally Different Candidates

Build one candidate from each axis. Every pair must differ in at least two of these properties: page topology, value and quiet-space distribution, or the mechanism carrying the meaning. Palette, texture, or medium changes alone do not constitute a different candidate.

### Relation-Led

Let posture, interval, gaze, reach, overlap, or separation determine the focal assembly and relational echo. Environmental material supports the relationship rather than becoming scenery.

### Environment-Led

Let snow, water, foliage, architecture, shadow, wind, ground, or light become the main field that presses on, shelters, separates, or carries the focal assembly.

### Sequence-Led

Let a visible action or temporal change become a small set of evolving states. Vary scale, density, or material across the sequence and preserve a clear origin, transition, and unresolved or completed endpoint.

## Candidate Fingerprint

Write each candidate with the same fields:

- `basis`: observed cues and Style Profile properties supporting the direction;
- `meaning_core`: subject count, relationship, event, and necessary counterpart;
- `evidence_atoms`: posture, interval, color partition, material cue, direction, and environmental pressure;
- `form_translations`: observed cue, abstraction operation, and page role;
- `role_graph`: focal assembly, relational echo, environmental field, and counterform;
- `page_forces`: axes, compression, release, enclosure, crossing, or drift;
- `scale_ladder`: broad masses, middle structures, and fine evidence;
- `active_void`: what shapes each quiet region and what tension it carries;
- `material_stack`: stock, dominant process, subordinate process, overlaps, and edge variation;
- `value_map`: darkest, middle, lightest, and focal contrast relationships;
- `color_jobs`: job of every non-neutral color;
- `identity_filter`: details generalized and semantically necessary features retained;
- `anti_cliche`: likely generic symbol or minimal formula and how the direction prevents it;
- `retry_trigger`: the single visible failure that would justify regeneration.
- `explicit_override`: exact user-requested extreme-minimal or other structural exception; record which role or density gate it waives and which semantic, active-void, identity, and material gates remain. Use `none` when absent.

## Selection Order

Apply hard gates first:

1. subject count, relationship, posture, and necessary counterpart remain readable;
2. focal assembly, relational echo, environmental field, and counterform are each visually locatable; they may share a boundary but the focal assembly cannot absorb all other roles, an unshaped blank area cannot count as field or counterform, and one connector cannot satisfy the entire role graph. A recorded explicit extreme-minimal override may waive only named role or density requirements while preserving semantic and active-void gates;
3. broad, middle, and fine information form a hierarchy;
4. quiet regions are shaped by page forces rather than left unused;
5. every major motif has source evidence and no exemplar content has leaked.

Reject any candidate summarized as "one silhouette, one line, blank paper" unless the user explicitly requests extreme minimalism and the source relationship still survives. Rank survivors for semantic fidelity, specificity, tension, material coherence, and generation risk. At least two candidates must pass the gates. If fewer survive, build a genuinely different replacement candidate and test it; if two survivors still cannot be established, stop before generation. Freeze the winner and retain the runner-up.

## Failure Routing

- **surface failure**: color balance, edge breakup, grain, or local density is wrong while structure works. Regenerate once with the same full specification and one emphasized fix.
- **structure failure**: generic iconography, missing counterpart, dead void, weak scale hierarchy, or formulaic minimalism dominates. Compile a separate runner-up Generation Blueprint and Illustration Score from the immutable Evidence Capsule, Style Profile, and runner-up fingerprint; do not include winning-direction fields or patch the failed structure with decoration.
- **meaning failure**: subject count, relationship, posture, or event becomes unreadable. Restore the complete semantic locks; if one regeneration cannot verify them, mark `FAILED`.

A core specificity or structure failure cannot be classified as `LIMITED` merely because the surface is attractive.
